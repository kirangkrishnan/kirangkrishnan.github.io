I am a doctoral researcher in the [Nonlinear Systems and Control Group](https://www.aalto.fi/en/department-of-electrical-engineering-and-automation/nonlinear-systems-and-control) at Aalto University, Finland, under the supervision of Shankar Deka. My research interests lie broadly at the intersection of machine learning (ML) and control theory. In particular, my research focuses on data-driven control strategies using Koopman operator theory.

I received my B.Tech. degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from the National Institute of Technology Calicut, India. After my bachelor's, I worked at Bosch India Ltd. and Swadheshy Polymers for about three years. I then completed my M.Sc. (Tech.) at Aalto University, Finland, specializing in Control, Robotics and Autonomous Systems with a minor in ML.

## Past experiences
* **Teaching assistant** for Digital and Optimal Control, Control and Automation, and Nonlinear Control Design and Analysis
* **Research assistant** at Cyber-Physical System group led by Prof. Dominik Baumann and Intelligent Robotics group led by Prof. Ville Kyrki
* **Technical Consultant** at Swadheshy Polymers, India
* **Technical Management Trainee** at Bosch India Limited, Bangalore
* **Team Leader** Robotics Interest Group - NIT Calicut, India
